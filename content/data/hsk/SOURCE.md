## Corrections to the transcription

Applied by `data:import` from `hsk/corrections.yaml`:

| Level | Fix | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | word 示 → **表示** | PDF p. 48: L2 #29 is 表示 biǎoshì |
| 2 | add **面²** (noodles; flour) after 面¹ | PDF p. 53: #377 面¹ (名、量), #378 面² (名); the transcription merged them |
| 2 | 不一会儿 pinyin yīhuǐr5 → bù yīhuìr | stray digit; PDF: bù yíhuìr |
| 2 | character 人 → **入** | 人 is already level 1; the list order (如 rú, ?, 色 sè) and the L2 words 入口, 收入, 进入 need 入 rù |
| 6 | 铺 pù → **pū** | PDF p. 102: L6 #634 铺 pū (to spread), after 扑 pū |
| 7–9 | 鑾 → **攀**, 恰然自得 → **怡然自得**, 引人人胜 → **引人入胜** | OCR slips; the list's pinyin order confirms each |

Systematic fixes in `data:import` itself: erhua written `r5` becomes `r`; missing apostrophes are
restored (kěài → kě'ài) by choosing the split whose syllable count equals the character count; a
grammar code closed with `］` instead of `】` is read (this was why levels 4 and 6 showed 75 and 66
points); a word listed twice with the same reading (批 verb, 批 measure word) becomes two senses with
their own ids.

## Pinyin conventions

- `py` holds **citation tones**: 一 is yī and 不 is bù here, and neutral tones are unmarked. The
  standard itself prints spoken tones for 一 and 不 (búcuò, yíhuìr); the app derives those with
  `lib/zh/sandhi.ts`, and lessons author them as spoken (CLAUDE.md §5.2).
- A space inside `py` marks a separable or phrase-like entry (帮忙 bāng máng; the standard prints
  bāng//máng). Content checks compare pinyin without spaces.
- Where the transcription and the standard disagree only on a neutral tone (白天 báitian / báitiān),
  the transcription's form is kept; content checks accept either.

## Handwriting characters per rung

The standard publishes its handwriting list by band, not by level. A rung may ask learners to trace a
character when it is on the handwriting list of its band or a lower one **and** is a character of the
rung's level or below (`lib/content/hsk.ts`, `handwritingUpTo`). For Easy 1 that is 273 characters:
the elementary-band handwriting characters that are HSK 1 characters. The Dasar rungs use the same set.
